Stable inputs

Use a unique id or testID for every controlled native input.

<TextInput id="email-input" value={email} onChangeText={setEmail} />
<Slider id="volume-slider" value={volume}
  minimumValue={0} maximumValue={100} step={10}
  onValueChange={setVolume} />

The slider is explicitly left-to-right and native controls are detached before full tree updates to prevent logical-parent errors.
